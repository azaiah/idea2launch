      How much does it cost to turn an idea into a working prototype?

      An Idea Blueprint is $299. A working Prototype, with a live website, admin dashboard and one working feature, is $799. Prototype Plus, with up to three features, is $1,499. Prices are fixed and paid up front through Square. For comparison, agencies commonly quote $5,000 to $150,000 for a full MVP.

      How fast will I see my idea working?

      Blueprints arrive in 24–48 hours. Prototypes take 3–5 business days and Plus takes 5–7, counted from when your scope is confirmed (usually the same or next business day).

      What's the difference between a prototype and a finished app?

      A prototype is the first working version of your idea: a real, live website and dashboard where one to three features actually work. It's built to show, test and pitch. It isn't a production platform with card payments, large user bases, complex integrations or regulated data like health records. We can quote that next phase once you've seen your prototype.

      Do I need to be technical?

      No. You describe the idea in plain words, and we handle the design, code and setup, then walk you through it on a call.

      Who owns my idea and the work?

      You own your idea. What you submit is confidential and never reused or shared. Once you've paid in full, the website content and design we make for you are yours. Details are in our service terms.

      What if my idea is too big for the package?

      Every idea gets a fit check within one business day of payment, before we start. If it doesn't fit, we'll recommend the right package (and you only pay the difference) or refund you in full.

      Why not just use a DIY AI app builder?

      DIY builders are cheap, but you're the designer, the tester and the person who fixes things at midnight. With Idea2Launch, developers who build portals for real businesses do it for you, with a fixed price, a deadline and a walkthrough.

      What happens after delivery?

      You keep the demo link to share. If you want it to stay online and get small updates, Launch Care starts at $99/month. When you're ready for the full version, we'll quote it from your roadmap.

      Can I pay in installments?

      Packages are paid up front, which is how we keep prices this low and turnaround this fast. Square Checkout may offer buy-now-pay-later options such as Afterpay or Cash App Pay, depending on your eligibility.
