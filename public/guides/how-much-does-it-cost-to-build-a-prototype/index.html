How much does it cost to build a prototype in 2026?

By Azaiah Wood, Developer & Solutions Architect at DataIsData. Updated October 24, 2026.

The short answer

A working prototype (a live website, a back-office dashboard and one feature that really works) costs $799 with Idea2Launch and takes 3–5 business days. A written concept and mockup (a blueprint) is $299. A full MVP from a development agency typically costs $5,000 to $150,000 and takes months.

What drives the price up

  • Number of features that must work. Each working feature (bookings, payments, messaging) adds design, code and testing.
  • User accounts and roles. Logins, permissions and multiple user types multiply the work.
  • Integrations. Payments, calendars, CRMs and third-party APIs each add setup and failure cases.
  • Regulated data. Health, financial or children's data brings security and compliance requirements that can double or triple cost.
  • Native mobile apps. Separate iOS and Android builds cost far more than a mobile-ready website.

Why a prototype first is the smart money move

The key point is this: most ideas change after real people see them. Spending $799 to put a working version in front of customers, partners or a lender tells you what to build before you commit $10,000 or more. Your prototype's roadmap then becomes the spec for the full build, so nothing is wasted.

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest way to get a working prototype?

The cheapest done-for-you option is a fixed-scope prototype service. Idea2Launch builds a live website, admin dashboard and one working feature for $799. DIY AI builders cost less in money but more in your time.

How much does an MVP cost in 2026?

Published 2026 industry pricing puts most MVPs between $5,000 and $150,000, with bare-bones single-feature builds around $5,000 and regulated products such as HIPAA healthtech reaching $150,000–$350,000 or more.

Is a prototype the same as an MVP?

No. A prototype proves the idea works and can be shown to people; an MVP is the smallest version real customers can use and pay for at scale. Many founders build a prototype first to validate demand before paying for an MVP.

How long does it take to build a prototype?

A focused prototype with one working feature can be delivered in 3–5 business days. A full MVP from an agency usually takes two to six months.
