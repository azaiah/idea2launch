Idea2Launch service terms

Last updated October 24, 2026. These terms apply to Idea2Launch packages sold by DataIsData, LLC ("DataIsData", "we").

1. What you're buying

Idea2Launch packages are fixed-scope prototypes and blueprints, not production software.

  • Idea Blueprint ($299): written concept outline, feature recommendations, one visual mockup and a roadmap. No deployed website.
  • Idea2Launch Prototype ($799): one landing page, one light admin dashboard, one working feature, a private demo link, a walkthrough and one revision round.
  • Idea2Launch Plus ($1,499): a polished multi-section website, dashboard, up to three working features, an expanded roadmap and two revision rounds.

2. What's not included

Unless agreed in writing, packages exclude payment processing, complex third-party integrations, regulated or sensitive data (for example health, financial or children's data), advanced security hardening, native mobile apps, custom domains and production-grade hosting. Prototypes may use sample data.

3. Payment and fit guarantee

Packages are paid in full up front through Square. Within one business day of payment we review your submission. If your idea doesn't fit the package you bought, we'll recommend the right package (you pay only the difference) or refund you in full. Once we confirm scope and you approve it, work begins and the package is non-refundable except where we fail to deliver the agreed scope.

Blueprint credit: if you buy an Idea Blueprint and upgrade to a Prototype or Plus for the same idea within 30 days, the $299 is credited toward it.

4. Timelines

Delivery windows (24–48 hours, 3–5 or 5–7 business days) start when your scope is confirmed and any information we need from you is received. Priority build moves your project to the front of our queue; it doesn't change the scope.

5. Revisions

A revision round is one consolidated list of changes within the agreed scope, sent within 14 days of delivery. New features or changes outside the scope are quoted separately.

6. Ownership and confidentiality

Your idea is yours. We keep your submission confidential and don't share or reuse your concept. Once you've paid in full, the content and design created specifically for your project are yours. DataIsData keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates and reusable components, and grants you a license to use them as part of your delivered prototype. We may mention that we worked with you only with your permission.

7. Hosting after delivery

We keep your demo online for 30 days after delivery. Ongoing hosting and small updates are available through Launch Care, from $99/month, under separate terms.

8. Limits of liability

Prototypes are provided for demonstration and testing. To the extent permitted by law, DataIsData's total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid for the package.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms? Reply to any email from DataIsData about your order.
