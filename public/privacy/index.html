Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026.

What we collect

When you use the idea builder or submit the intake form we collect what you type (your idea and project details) and your contact details (name, email and optional phone). We also record which page or campaign link brought you to us.

How we use it

  • To scope, build and deliver your project and to contact you about it.
  • To improve Idea2Launch.

We don't sell your information or share your idea with anyone outside DataIsData, except service providers who help us run the service (Netlify for hosting and form storage, Square for payments, and, if enabled, an AI provider to draft the instant concept preview). Each processes data only to provide its service.

Payments

Card details are entered on Square's secure checkout page. DataIsData never sees or stores your full card number.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by replying to any email from DataIsData.
