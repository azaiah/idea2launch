# Idea2Launch launch kit

Everything Tony and Azaiah need to start posting and closing. Links assume the site is live at
`https://idea2launch-dataisdata.netlify.app`. The short link `/idea` goes straight to the intake form, with tracking.

**Positioning in one line:** *The idea you've been sitting on, live in five days.* Fixed price, done for you by real developers, with a dashboard included.

**What makes people buy (use these in every post):**
1. **They can see it before they pay.** The homepage builder shows their own idea as a mini-site and dashboard in seconds. Every post should push people to *try it*, not just "DM me."
2. **A price they can say yes to today.** $799 against $5,000–$150,000 agency quotes.
3. **Speed.** 3–5 business days.
4. **No risk.** The fit guarantee gives a full refund before work starts if the idea doesn't fit.
5. **Real work behind it.** Link the live portfolio (Majestic Contracting portal, Loc'd In Vanity, New Life Deliverance Tabernacle).

Keep the scarcity honest: "first 10 customers get introductory pricing" is true. The counter on the site only moves when someone actually pays.
